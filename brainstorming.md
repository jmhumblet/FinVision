# Brainstorming Ideas based on Product Definition

Based on the core goals of FinVision (Financial Clarity, Predictive Modeling, AI Automation) from `/conductor/product.md`, here are 5 potential features:

1. **Interactive Debt Payoff Strategist**
   - *Concept:* A dedicated module to manage debt, featuring Snowball vs. Avalanche strategy comparison and interactive payoff visualization.
   - *Value:* Directly addresses the MVP requirement for a "Debt Payoff Strategist". Provides clear, predictive models for users wanting to become debt-free.

2. **AI Paycheck Routing Assistant**
   - *Concept:* An intelligent assistant that analyzes incoming paychecks and suggests optimal routing of funds across expenses, debt, and savings goals.
   - *Value:* Enhances the AI Automation goal by taking the guesswork out of how to distribute income.

3. **Proactive Scenario Discovery**
   - *Concept:* An AI-driven feature that analyzes the user's financial trajectory and automatically suggests beneficial 'What-If' scenarios (e.g., "What if you moved 5% of your checking to a high-yield savings account?").
   - *Value:* Strengthens Predictive Modeling by making it proactive rather than user-initiated.

4. **Life Event Cost Simulators**
   - *Concept:* AI-guided 'What-If' templates for common life events (e.g., buying a house, having a baby, getting a dog) to auto-generate expected costs and scenario adjustments.
   - *Value:* Reduces the friction of creating complex scenarios from scratch.

5. **AI Spending Anomaly Detection**
   - *Concept:* Proactively monitors transaction history and projected cash flow to detect and alert users of unusual spending patterns or forgotten subscriptions.
   - *Value:* Improves Financial Clarity and acts as a safety net for users.

## Iteration and Selection

When considering which of these features would be most useful for clients, we must weigh user impact against the product's MVP goals:
- **AI Paycheck Routing** and **Anomaly Detection** are excellent automation tools, but they rely heavily on accurate, historical transaction data, which might not be fully mature yet.
- **Proactive Scenario Discovery** and **Life Event Cost Simulators** add great value to the 'What-If' engine, but are secondary to giving users the tools to solve their immediate financial pain points.
- **Interactive Debt Payoff Strategist** stands out as the most critical missing piece. It is explicitly listed as a Key Feature (MVP) in the product definition. Debt management is a primary concern for the target audience (individual consumers managing budgets). Providing a visual, comparative tool (Snowball vs. Avalanche) delivers immediate, tangible value by showing a clear path to financial freedom.

**Selected Idea:** Interactive Debt Payoff Strategist.
